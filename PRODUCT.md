# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS: one `index.html` with inline CSS, no JavaScript, no framework, no build step. Hosted on Vercel at aryanhaghighi.com (auto-deploys main). Under 40 KB total, not counting a font file (raised from 20 KB on 2026-11-21 for the portrait: AVIF 5 KB at 2x, JPEG fallback 11 KB).

## Users

SaaS founders and AI agency owners who got a cold email or LinkedIn message from Aryan Haghighi and click through to check he is a real, serious engineer. They stay 5 to 10 seconds.

## Product Purpose

A one-screen holding page that replaces the Namecheap parking page until the real site exists. In 5 to 10 seconds it must answer: who is this, what does he do, how do I reach him. Success = the visitor trusts the cold message enough to reply.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One screen, no scrolling on desktop. Works at 320 px wide with a 16 px side margin and no horizontal scroll.
- Copy is fixed. Use only:
  - Name: Aryan Haghighi
  - Line 1: AI Engineer
  - Line 2: I ship AI agents into production, with evals that prove they work.
  - Line 3 (smaller, quieter): Building autonomous drones.
  - Portrait: portrait-256/384 (.avif + .jpg) from ~/Pictures/aryan-pfp.jpg, above the name.
  - Links: email aryan@aryanhaghighi.com (visible as the address, mailto), LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/aryan-haghighi-24b779304/, GitHub https://github.com/CipherDriftX
- No taglines, greetings, "coming soon", countdowns, or calls to action. Missing content is asked for, never invented.
- Meta: title "Aryan Haghighi, AI Engineer · production AI agents + evals", description from line 2, Open Graph + Twitter tags with a 1200x630 og:image, theme-color, inline SVG favicon.

## Brand Commitments

- Always the full name "Aryan Haghighi". Never "Aryan" alone as a short name, and never the initials "AH" as a monogram, favicon or logo (both can read as hate-group code in the US and EU). Favicon is a plain geometric mark, no letters.
- Voice: calm, sharp, quiet. A serious engineer's personal page, not a startup landing page.

## Evidence on Hand

LinkedIn and GitHub profiles only. No case studies, clients, testimonials or metrics exist on this page and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Answer who / what / how-to-reach in one eye path, top to bottom.
- Fewer elements beat more. Every element must earn its place in a 5-second scan.
- Trust comes from restraint and correctness (fast, accessible, valid), not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes (prefers-color-scheme). Semantic HTML. Links look like links, with visible keyboard focus.
