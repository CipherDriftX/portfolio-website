## Scope and mode

The whole site: home (`src/pages/index.astro`), case study pages (`src/pages/work/[slug].astro`), note pages, 404. Home first screen is Persuade; case study and note pages are Read.

## Audience and job

Founders and agency owners who got a cold email or LinkedIn message, plus hiring managers for AI engineer roles. 5 to 10 seconds on the first screen: who, what, how to reach. Action = reply, or click email or LinkedIn. Proof on hand: the photo, LinkedIn, GitHub. No clients, metrics or reviews exist; drafts hold their places and never ship. Owner's theme (2026-11-22): dark theme and fighter jets, Darkstar and SR-72 above all, "but dont make it look like im an aerospace engineering looking for aerospace work, just use the theme".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Aryan Haghighi shown the way a great aviation hall shows its one black aircraft: hard light, a precise label, nothing else. Refuses the photo-beside-name hero on a glowing dark gradient, neon edges, HUD and cockpit symbols, stencil costume.
OWN-WORLD: Cool hall black, lit graphite pools in two states only (lit or unlit, hard edges, no haze, no blooms, no grain), bone label text, one heat-amber accent for links, focus and selection. Archivo Expanded for the name and section titles like hall lettering; Hanken Grotesk for label text, tabular figures. A matte black jet, the site mark drawn large, hangs under the ceiling with one facet catching the spot. The photo is black and white. No boxes, rules or dividers: grouping by pools of light.
STORY: In five seconds the visitor sees who (the name, his photo in its spot), what (AI Engineer, the claim) and how to reach him (links on the label). Scrolling walks the hall bay by bay: How I work, then exhibits as they exist, each with the identical label; Contact closes the hall.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900. The hung jet high left, nose up and right, about a third of the width. A spot beam whose left edge follows the jet's blended leading-edge curve falls from the ceiling onto a hard-edged pool holding the photo, right, about 45% wide. Museum label left on the page frame: name in Archivo Expanded capitals up to 6rem on two lines, AI Engineer, claim, drone line, then email, LinkedIn, GitHub. Top bar: mark left, section links right. The How I work bay is lit at the fold. Phones: photo pool on top, jet small upper left, label below, links on the first screen. Signature, lights up: on load the hall's spots snap on in order (jet, photo, label) as hard cuts within 600 ms; each bay's spot snaps on as it enters the view, CSS only; reduced motion: all lit from the start. Cited adaptations: (1) the jet hangs nose up with a 15° lean toward the spot, not on its side: past about 30° the two-facet mark reads as a paper dart; it hangs from the ceiling, rising into the home bar's empty left end, lit facet bone and shade facet #24272d, and fills the dark above the label: its box is about 16% of the width at 1440x900 (234 px), 15% at 1920x1080, 11% at 1280x720 (138 px); a fifth or a third would push the links past the fold. (2) On the home page the top bar leaves the mark out: the hung jet is the one jet in the room; other pages keep the mark as the home link. (3) Phones: the print zooms 1.1 from its left edge and the spot's edge starts at 32% of the width, so his face clears the curve; under 720px tall only the first bay's lit edge shows at the fold and its title starts below it. (5) Landscape phones (wide layout under 32rem tall): the jet is left out, the spot's edge lands at 56% on the tall curve, and only the first bay's lit edge shows at the fold. (6) The decisive beat: How I work's title rises 0.46em out of its bay, so the bay's hard top edge cuts through the middle of the letters; on short and landscape phones, when it is the first bay, the title stays inside the bay so the fold never slices it. (4) Wide screens: the spot's edge lands on the floor at 44% of the width and the label stays left of it.
