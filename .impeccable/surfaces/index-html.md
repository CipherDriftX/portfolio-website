## Scope and mode

index.html, the whole site: a one-screen holding page. Mode: Persuade (the visitor decides whether a cold message is from a real engineer and replies).

## Audience and job

Founders who got a cold email/DM. 5-10 s visit. Must answer who, what, how to reach. Action = the three contact links. Proof on hand: none beyond LinkedIn/GitHub; nothing may be invented.

## Directions considered

1. Title card (chosen): left-aligned block at optical center, two-tone heading, statement, quiet drone line, underlined links.
2. Plain document: default-looking text page like old engineer homepages. Rejected: reads unfinished to a founder judging polish.
3. Poster split: name top-left, links bottom-left, empty middle. Rejected: eye jumps ~600 px from who to how-to-reach; fragile on short laptop screens.
The seed roll dealt six catalog challengers (fan shrine, Saul Bass titles, cathode gauze, cutting bench, accretion disk, alphabet storm); all declined, they break the brief's bans on motion, glow and extra copy. Raise from Saul Bass titles: hard asymmetry, one block, the right of the screen left empty. Raise from cathode gauze: no box, rule or divider anywhere; grouping by space alone. Brief pins the process (author picks), so the roll did not pick.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A name, a claim and three links set like a film title card, held left with the screen empty to the right. Refuses the centered startup hero with tagline, button and gradient.
OWN-WORLD: Hanken Grotesk only, weights 400 and 600. Ink on neutral near-white, or bone on near-black in dark. One accent, international orange (aerospace safety color), only on link underlines and the focus ring. No boxes, rules, shadows, icons or images.
STORY: Visitor reads the name, sees "AI Engineer", reads the one-sentence claim, notices the drone line, and clicks email or LinkedIn. Trust comes from restraint and correctness.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900. Block left edge on a centered 72rem frame (x of about 144), vertically just above center. Name 80px/600 ink; "AI Engineer" same size in muted grey directly under it; 48px gap; claim 24px ink; 12px gap; drone line 16px muted; 48px gap; links row 16px with icons, orange underlines (review round 1: links share the 16px body step; 17px would add a fourth size 1px from the third; ink colour, icon and orange underline set them apart from the muted drone line). Right ~55% empty. Primary action = links row. Phones: name scales up to about 85-95% of the line (clamp 40px floor, 7vw slope) so the title card holds at 320-480px; no height-based units (they shrink text under zoom).
FORM: Title card, position 1 of my own ordered list of three; seed:505e1fad4de7d5b5
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
